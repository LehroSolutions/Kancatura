# Lehro Solutions Marketing Strategy

Create compelling marketing content and strategic plans for Lehro Solutions.

## When to Use
- Marketing strategy development
- Content creation (blog posts, social media, ads)
- Campaign planning
- Competitive analysis
- Brand positioning
- Launch announcements

## Steps

### 1. Understand the Goal
- What's the objective? (awareness, leads, sales)
- Who's the target audience?
- What's the timeline?
- What's the budget (if any)?

### 2. Position Lehro Solutions
Lehro Solutions is:
- AI-native company
- Open-source flagship: LehroForge
- Bridge between business and technical
- "We don't adapt to AI. We're born in it."

Key messaging pillars:
- Speed (AI-powered workflows = 10x faster)
- Quality (AI + human oversight = better results)
- Transparency (open-source, community-driven)
- Innovation (always at the frontier)

### 3. Content Strategy
```
Blog (2x/week)
  └─ AI industry insights
  └─ LehroForge tutorials
  └─ Behind-the-scenes development

Social Media (daily)
  └─ X/Twitter: Tech insights, product updates
  └─ LinkedIn: Business-focused content
  └─ GitHub: Open-source activity

Community (ongoing)
  └─ Discord engagement
  └─ Tech conference participation
  └─ Open-source contributions
```

### 4. Campaign Types
- Product launch campaigns
- Developer recruitment campaigns
- Thought leadership content
- Case study development
- Community growth initiatives

### 5. Content Creation Guidelines
- Tech casual tone (sharp but not stiff)
- Lead with value, not hype
- Show, don't tell (demo > claims)
- Authentic voice (no corporate jargon)
- Data-backed claims (use metrics)

## Output Formats
- Blog posts (markdown format)
- Social media content calendars
- Email campaigns
- Landing page copy
- Pitch decks
- Competitive analysis reports

## LehroForge Marketing Focus
Since LehroForge is our flagship:
- Position as "API orchestration for the AI age"
- Target: developers, startups, AI teams
- Differentiate from: Zapier (legacy), Make (no AI focus)
- Emphasize: agentic workflows, security-first, open-source
